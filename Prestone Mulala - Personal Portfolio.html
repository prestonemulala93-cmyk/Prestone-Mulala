Biography & Vision

Integrating Clinical Excellence, Advocacy & Enterprise

I am a final-stage clinical medical student at the University of Zambia (UNZA) Ridgeway Campus, actively engaged in full-time clinical work at the premier University Teaching Hospital (UTH) in Lusaka. My clinical focus spans Surgery, Pediatrics, Internal Medicine, and Obstetrics & Gynecology.

As former UNZASU Ridgeway Campus President, I served as the primary executive representative for thousands of health sciences students, leading high-stakes negotiations with university administration and government bodies to secure improved learning infrastructure, health facilities, and student welfare policies.

In parallel, I established D4T1 Printing Services to supply key academic, legal, and corporate print solutions in Lusaka. Additionally, I author structured economic proposals—such as commercial livestock production models for national development initiatives—aimed at supporting sustainable food security across Zambia.
